Support

The Impostor · Español

Found a bug, have a question or an idea for the game? Write to daniel_peimbert@hotmail.com. If it’s a bug, tell us what you were doing and, if you can, send a screenshot.

How do you play?

Everyone gets the same secret word except the impostor. Players take turns saying one related word, and at the end the group votes on who to accuse. The full instructions are in the app, under “How to play”.

Which iPhones does it work on?

Any iPhone with iOS 26 or later.

How do I delete the saved names?

In New game, swipe each name to the left to remove it. To delete everything, uninstall the app.

Does it cost anything or have ads?

No. The game is free, with no ads and no subscription.
